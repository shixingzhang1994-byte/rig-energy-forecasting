\begin{abstract}
Abrupt drilling-load transitions can exhaust storage reserve before additional
diesel generation becomes model-reachable under commitment and ramping constraints.
We evaluate a reserve--SOC supervisory model predictive control architecture
(RSS) that couples validation-residual load scenarios, conditional value at
risk, discrete generator commitment, and storage-reserve supervision. In a
prospectively frozen 12-seed simulation study, RSS reduces mean expected energy
not served (EENS) by 5.24 kWh relative to rule-based control, 15.59 kWh relative
to point-forecast mixed-integer predictive control, and 5.49 kWh relative to
risk-adaptive residual-CVaR control. Seed-cluster bootstrap 95\% intervals are
$[-9.43,-1.29]$, $[-21.45,-9.98]$, and $[-8.70,-2.80]$ kWh, respectively; all
three contrasts remain significant after Holm correction. Mean realized
operating cost is nevertheless 153.83--280.17 yuan higher, establishing a
reliability--cost trade-off rather than joint improvement; no equal-cost sweep
was performed. A public-data audit spans eight dataset families and covers load
forecasting, BESS behavior, and drilling-process timing. Confirmatory forecasting
on eight measured electrical series from three dataset families improves on
persistence for 5/8 series at 1 min and 8/8 at both 15 and 60 min. Two real BESS
datasets show 95.28--97.98\% power--SOC directional consistency. A pooled
coarse-state diagnostic for two public drilling records yields shorter dwell
times than the simulator (median 2 versus 43 min), although 85.39\% of the
Energistics labels are unknown. The results therefore support a conditional
reliability advantage in the frozen simulation and bounded real-data component
checks, but not economic superiority, closed-loop field efficacy, or direct
transfer of the simulator's temporal assumptions.
\end{abstract}

\begin{highlights}
\item Storage-reserve supervision coordinates batteries and model-reachable generation.
\item Frozen 12-seed tests show lower EENS than three matched references.
\item Reliability gains require higher realized operating cost.
\item Real-load tests improve on persistence at 15 and 60 min in 8/8 series.
\item Real BESS and drilling records expose both support and transfer limits.
\end{highlights}
